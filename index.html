Build philosophy

Five principles behind the work.

  1. 01

    HR is a systems discipline.

    Requests, approvals, and records are structured data. I design the systems that carry them cleanly.

  2. 02

    Build where people already work.

    Slack, Google Workspace, and the core HR system teams already open every day, rather than another login.

  3. 03

    Define success, then start small.

    Every build begins with the operational question it has to answer, then ships the smallest surface that answers it.

  4. 04

    Numbers you can defend.

    One written definition for every metric, so Finance, People, and executive leadership read the same figure.

  5. 05

    Every tool has an owner.

    A named owner, clear documentation, and explicit access boundaries, so the system outlasts the person who built it.

Selected systems

Five builds, one operating model.

Each system below is a lightweight web application built on Google Workspace, Slack, the core HR system, and payroll exports. Every build includes a short walkthrough, and four include interactive demos you can explore directly.

01 · The Rooftop

One hire, from request to day 90.

The company intranet: home, people directory, onboarding, performance reviews, and Petunia, the embedded assistant. The walkthrough follows a single hiring request end to end: intake, division and finance sign-off, plan updates, recruiting handoff, onboarding setup, manager preparation, day one, pulse check-ins, and the metrics that close the loop.

In productionEmbedded AI resolved 93% of 120+ employee questions without escalation in week one; paired People Ops service desk handled 1,700+ requests in 2025 at a 4.9 CSAT.

03 · Viewpoint Planning

Model the year from live seats.

Annual workforce planning built directly inside Viewpoint. Start from active seats and fully loaded costs, add or move seats and watch budget impact update immediately, compare scenarios, check incoming requests against the plan, and step the org chart forward month by month. Petunia drafts structured plan changes from natural conversation.

In productionReplaces disconnected headcount spreadsheets with live seat inventory, fully loaded budget modeling, and capacity ratios shared across Finance and division leaders.

04 · Safety Intake

One branching form, one triage queue.

Injuries, vehicle incidents, property damage, and near misses routed from a single branching intake form into one triage queue. Active hazards and emergency care surface automatically, every filing generates an immediate summary, and a structured case report exports in one click.

In productionUnifies multi-state field incident reporting into a single auditable queue with automated triage alerts for open hazards and emergency care.

05 · OT Analytics

Every overtime dollar, reconciled.

Payroll overtime exports matched against the active census, then rolled up by manager, department, and individual. Tracks hourly population coverage and year-over-year trends, flags repeat earners, first-time overtime, and top-ten spend concentration, and includes a full-screen executive briefing mode.

In productionTurns raw payroll overtime exports into recurring executive briefings highlighting hourly coverage, repeat-earner trends, and top-ten spend concentration.
